Gold dots = "something in here is non-default" — on tabs and on groups, so a stranger's project reads at a glance. Bypass pills A/B a whole group without losing values (Resolve's enable toggle). Clip metadata lives in the always-visible header; actions live in the footer, out of the property scroll.

Every row keeps one anatomy: keyframe diamond · label · slider · value. Same order everywhere, so muscle memory transfers between tabs.
What moves where (video clip):
  • Opacity + blend mode leave Transform and join track matte in Mix — compositing gets one home instead of two.
  • Crop joins Transform (it is a spatial property), Mask gets its own tab (CapCut precedent; it is the thing people hunt for).
  • Clip Info stops being a section — name, type, resolution, fps, duration in the sticky header; the long metadata table moves behind the ⋯ menu.
  • Commit Render and Reveal become footer actions — they are verbs, not properties.
  • Expansion state and defaults persist per tab+group exactly as today (same localStorage key shape).
